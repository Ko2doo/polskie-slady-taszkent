# Privacy Policy — Polskie Ślady Taszkent

_Last updated: 20 June 2026_

**Polskie Ślady Taszkent** ("the App") is a free, open‑source reference guide with
offline GPS navigation. We respect your privacy: the App is designed to work fully
offline and **does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data**.

## Data we collect

**None.** The App contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no user
accounts. We do not operate any server that receives your data.

## Location

The App can use your device's location to show your position on the map and to guide
you to nearby heritage sites. This happens **entirely on your device**. Your location
is **never** collected, logged, stored, or sent to us or to any third party. You can
use the App without granting location access; you simply won't see your own position
on the map. You can revoke location permission at any time in your device settings.

## Map data

Maps are rendered from offline tiles bundled with the App (map data ©
OpenStreetMap contributors, rendered with MapLibre GL, packaged with Protomaps and
PMTiles). Browsing the map does not require an internet connection and does not send
your activity anywhere.

## Links to third‑party services

The App can open a selected place in Google Maps at your request. When you follow such
a link, you leave the App and the third‑party service's own privacy policy applies.

## Children's privacy

The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone,
including children.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL.
